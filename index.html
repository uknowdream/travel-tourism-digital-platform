PLANNING, CLARIFIED

Better questions lead to better journeys.

This platform is a research and planning companion. Use the notes below to understand its tools and the checks that remain essential before booking.

What can I do with this platform?

Search destinations by name, region and experience; open destination details; save favourites; add up to five route stops; and generate a trip brief to copy or download.

Does the planner book hotels, flights or tours?

No. It creates a planning document in your browser. No reservation, supplier inventory, payment gateway or live availability service is connected.

Are sample routes fixed or geographically optimised itineraries?

No. They are editable starting concepts. Verify actual transport options, travel time, entry rules and seasonal conditions before combining destinations.

Is my saved list stored in an account?

No account is required. Favourites and route stops are saved locally in this browser when storage is available. They are not synchronised across devices.

How do I know travel information is current?

Destination descriptions are editorial inspiration rather than live alerts. Check official immigration, government advisory, attraction, transport and accommodation sources for current requirements.
